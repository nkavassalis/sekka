# Caer Dhul — location brief

> FICTIONAL LOCATION — invented for this example.

## What it is
A town built into and on top of a shelf of black rock, three weeks' ride from
the nearest thaw. Six hundred hearths, one glacier-wall, and the Assay
Council's northernmost tax office. Getting in is easy; getting *out* costs a
stamp from the Gate Wardens, and stamps cost.

## Key sites
- **The Last Hearth** (Hearthwright house) — last inn before the spire road.
  Seraine Ashfoot keeps it. Stew fixes most things; coin fixes the rest.
- **Assay House** — token licensing, heat-tax, and a waiting room designed to
  make healthy people feel guilty. Auditor **Bell** is new from the capital
  and *hungry* for a headline case.
- **The Drip** — the under-shelf warren where the stamps are cheaper and the
  answers aren't. Blackwater has two hooks in here; the Council pretends not
  to know where they are.
- **Gate Wardens' post** — outward stamps. A Council token buys speed; a
  Hearthwright word buys pity; neither buys both.

## Law, briefly
- Curfew when the spire *sings* (it means a storm is 6–10 hours out — trust
  it). Open flame in the streets during curfew: confiscated, twice, then
  the Drip gets you instead.
- Untokened frostweaving inside town limits is a Council offense. The
  Wardens look the other way for hearth-light and melted boots. Officially:
  they do not.

## Current rumors (drop them when the scene needs weather)
- The Cinder Road third burn was no accident, and an auditor is asking
  questions at the wrong tables.
- A barge southbound next week travels *unwrapped*, and someone paid to
  make sure of it.
- Something in the ice below the Drip was heard to knock. Probably pipes.
  Probably.
