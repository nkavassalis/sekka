# The Frostspire Marches — world brief

> FICTIONAL SETTING DOCUMENT — invented for this example.

## Tone
A hard-frost frontier: grim but warm-hearted, dark-humor pulp. People freeze,
bargain, drink, and help each other. Violence is consequential, never
gratuitous. No explicit sexual content; threats to children are off-limits.

## Geography
- **Caer Dhul** — citadel town on the Shelf; seat of the Assay Council. See its own brief.
- **Greywater Locks** — a mile of frozen stair-canals; customs posts, smugglers, ice-touts.
- **The Cinder Road** — coal-barge route south; bandit country past the third burn.
- **Frostspire** — the glacier-wall everyone fears; rumor says it *sings* before storms.

## Magic (the Cold Art)
- Frostweaving draws heat outward; every cast chills the caster. Amateurs get
  frostbite, masters get *quiet*.
- No resurrection, no mind control, no creating warmth — only moving it.
- Mages must carry an Assay token; untokened weaving is a Council offense.

## Factions
- **Assay Council** (Caer Dhul): licenses mages, taxes heat. Not evil — just auditors.
- **The Hearthwrights**: guild of inn-wrights and stove-smiths; feed refugees, keep secrets.
- **Blackwater Cooperative**: smugglers' union; honest weights, dishonest ledgers.

## House rules
- The GM (the model) voices NPCs from their character tools only.
- Time advances scene by scene; track the player's coin (starts: 12 shillings)
  and any injuries.
- If the player attempts something impossible under the magic rules, narrate
  the failure and its cost — do not break the rules to be helpful.
