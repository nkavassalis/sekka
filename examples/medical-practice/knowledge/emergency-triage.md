# Emergency Triage — Symptom to Action
> HYPOTHETICAL EXAMPLE POLICY — fictional practice. In a real deployment,
> this document would be reviewed by a clinician. Call emergency services
> for anything in the RED row before doing anything else.

## RED — call emergency services now (911/999/112), then notify on-site staff
- Chest pain/pressure, esp. radiating to arm/jaw, with sweating or nausea
- Difficulty breathing, stridor, blue lips
- Stroke signs (FAST): Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech change, Time —
  note the exact time symptoms started
- Severe bleeding not slowing after 10 min of direct pressure
- Suspected overdose: unresponsive, slow/absent breathing, pinpoint pupils
- Any seizure > 5 minutes or repeated seizures
- Anaphylaxis: swelling of tongue/throat after exposure

**Script:** "I'm calling an ambulance right now — stay on the line with me."
Do not let the caller hang up to "look something up". Do not advise driving
themselves in.

## YELLOW — same-day clinical contact (page the on-call provider now)
- Fever > 39.4°C / 103°F, or any fever in a chart flagged immunocompromised
- Abdominal pain > 6 hours, worsening, or with vomiting blood
- Missed dialysis window · suicidal thoughts with a plan (never leave the
  caller with "call later" — warm-transfer to the crisis line and confirm)
- Child: fever + stiff neck, or dehydration signs (no urine 12 h)

## GREEN — routine scheduling
Rashes without systemic symptoms · medication side-effect questions ·
follow-up results · minor sprains > 48 h old with normal weight-bearing.

**Front desk never gives a diagnosis in any row.** Action words only:
call an ambulance / provider will call you / here's the next slot.
