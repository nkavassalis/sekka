# Patient Check-In — Front Desk SOP
> HYPOTHETICAL EXAMPLE POLICY — Ridgeline Family Medicine is a fictional
> practice. Not medical, administrative, or compliance advice.

1. **Greet and verify identity**: full name + date of birth. Two-match rule:
   name+DOB must match the chart before any discussion of care.
2. **Photo ID** for new patients or patients flagged `ID-RECHECK`.
3. **Insurance card scan** — both sides, every visit. If the card differs
   from the chart, trigger the insurance-verification procedure.
4. **Intake tablet**: reason for visit, med-list confirmation, allergies.
   Patient signs the consent checkbox; staff initials counter-sign.
5. **Co-pay**: collect per fee schedule (see payment-processing procedure).
   Cannot pay today? Note `PMT-ARRANGE` — never turn a patient away.
6. **Delay notice**: if the provider is >15 min late, tell every waiting
   patient personally. Offer reschedule after 30.
7. **Escort**: temperature in chart, wheelchair if requested, introduce
   yourself to the clinical team by name.

Escalate to the practice manager: patient refuses any step, requests records
in person, or becomes distressed.
