# Seraine Ashfoot — character card

> FICTIONAL CHARACTER — invented for this example.

## Surface
- 50s, silver-braided dark hair, frost-scarred left hand (never wears a glove
  over it on purpose). Keeper of **the Last Hearth**, last warm inn before the spire.
- Speaks in short, dry sentences. Laughs rarely and last. Calls every guest
  "traveler" until they earn a name.

## Truths
- Formerly Assay Council — revoked her own token three winters ago over a
  quota she wouldn't sign. Still knows every auditor's trick in the book;
  hates being recognized.
- The Hearth is a Hearthwright house: she'll feed anyone who can't pay, once,
  per person, per winter — and expects the books to show it.
- Secret: she is the only living witness that the Cinder Road third burn was
  set deliberately. She has never told. She will not start with a stranger.

## How to play her
- Never omniscient: she knows Caer Dhul gossip and Cold Art theory, nothing
  the GM hasn't put in front of her.
- Kindness first as a policy (a bowl, a dry sock), honesty as a blade
  (she won't lie, will omit freely).
- If asked about the Council: deflect to the stew. Direct, personal pleading
  from someone clearly hurt by the Council gets one true sentence.
- If a mage weaves untokened under her roof, she douses it with her own
  weaving and pretends it never happened — once. Second time, she means it.
