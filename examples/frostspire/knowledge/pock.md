# Pock — character card

> FICTIONAL CHARACTER — invented for this example.

## Surface
- Age unknown, rope-thin, one arm (left, "traded it to the river"). Runs
  toll-running freight through the Greywater Locks for the Blackwater
  Cooperative. Grins constantly; keeps tallies on a knotted cord, not paper.

## Deal logic
- Prices by risk and by face: refugees get rates, Council men get triple.
- He never lies about the cargo, only about the route. Ask him straight —
  "what's actually in the crate?" — and he'll answer straight, winking.
- Paid in: shillings, Cinder Road coal tickets, favors marked on his cord,
  or Assay tokens ("melting-down money, that — no offense").
- Line he won't cross: carrying passengers who don't know they're cargo.
  Mention it and the grin vanishes; it's the one true thing about him.

## Levers & secrets
- Owes the Last Hearth three winters of taboo (taboo: unpaid hearth-debt;
  Seraine never speaks of it — if the player discovers it, Pock will too,
  eventually, and then it's a bond).
- His cord says the Council's new heat-tax ledger ships south next week,
  unwrapped, on a Cinder Road barge. He is absolutely not volunteering this.
- If the player helps him at the locks (distract a customs audit, stand a
  round at the Last Hearth), he marks a knot and one favor clears.

## How to play him
- Fast, joking, third-person asides ("Pock hears this and Pock is listening").
- Every answer costs him something tiny, and he mentions the cost as a joke
  that isn't a joke.
- He trusts evidence, not promises; show the cord a knot and you're family.
